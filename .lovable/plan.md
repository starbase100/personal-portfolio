# Ukiyo-e portfolio redesign

## Goal
Turn the existing continuous portfolio into a highly interactive, modern Ukiyo-e journey while preserving all current content, the day/night switch, accessibility, and the existing section order.

## What will change
- Restyle the full site with aged-paper texture, Prussian blue and indigo, vermilion, pale gold, and sumi-ink outlines.
- Rework the day and night landscapes into cohesive woodblock-print scenes inspired by the uploaded Great Wave reference, featuring sky, mountains, water, greenery, boats, sun or moon, and the travelling craft.
- Add an **Experimentation** section after About for future mini-projects, work photographs, and personal interests, without inventing entries.
- Add Experimentation to the floating navigation and preserve smooth anchor navigation.
- Give every major section the same layered “next sheet covers the previous sheet” transition now used between the opening landscape and Orientation.
- Make the travelling craft follow a continuous curved path, banking and rotating smoothly through turns instead of flipping abruptly.
- Add one distinct, cohesive woodblock-style artwork beside each existing Work project title, based only on its supplied topic.
- Recolor Research and About as distinct Ukiyo-e environments while retaining readable light and dark variants.
- Add restrained interactions: carved-border emphasis, wave/ripple responses, artwork reveals, cursor states, and gentle scroll entrances.

## Visual system
- **Headings:** Cormorant Garamond with calligraphic contrast.
- **Body:** Plus Jakarta Sans; JetBrains Mono remains for technical labels.
- **Light:** aged cream paper, linen, Prussian blue, vermilion, pale gold, sumi ink.
- **Dark:** deep indigo paper, moonlit blue, muted gold, pale ink, restrained vermilion.
- Borders remain sharp and ink-like; corners stay modest rather than pill-heavy, except the existing floating navigation.

## Content and accessibility
- Keep all existing biography, research, project, and contact wording unchanged.
- The Experimentation gallery will clearly be ready for future additions rather than showing invented work.
- Maintain keyboard focus, reduced-motion fallbacks, touch-friendly behavior, alt text, and readable contrast.

## Verification
- Check the complete journey on desktop and mobile.
- Confirm the theme switch, navigation, smooth craft path, artwork layout, section-cover transitions, and no horizontal gaps or overflow.
- Confirm the app builds cleanly and every image loads.
